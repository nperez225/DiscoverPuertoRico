Welcome

Welcome to Puerto Rico, or as many call it, "La Isla Del Encanto " ("The Island of Enchantment "). This Caribbean island is filled with so many beautiful beaches, amazing food, and vibrant culture. Whether you're looking for an adventure, to learn about a new culture, or simply to relax, Puerto Rico has everything you need!

Fun Facts!

  • Capital: San Juan
  • Languages Spoken: Spanish & English
  • Currency: U.S. Dollars
  • Time Zone: Atlantic Standard Time
  • For U.S. Citizens: No passport required
  • Puerto Rico has almost 300 beaches
  • Home of "El Yunque", the only tropical rainforest in the U.S. National Forest System.
