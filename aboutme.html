Meet The Developer

Background

Hi! My name is Nallely, I am 27 years old, and I live in Chicago, Illinois! I am currently a Computer Science student who loves to learn, create, and all things tech! This website was created to share with everyone why Puerto Rico is a really special place for me. I hope I can convince you to experience the beauty for yourself.

I come from a multicultural family. My mom is Mexican, and my dad is from Puerto Rico. I grew up surrounded by both cultures and learned about all the different traditions, food, and music that make each one unique. My first language was Spanish, and I learned English in school. Sometimes it can be a struggle to figure out where you fit in, in terms of your ethnicity. I was always "too Mexican " for the Puerto Ricans, and "too Puerto Rican " for the Mexicans. This caused a bit of an identity crisis for me growing up because I never felt like I belonged. As an adult now, and a mother of three, I finally feel like I belong. Seeing my kids also grow up in a multicultural home, exactly like I did, reminded me that both cultures make me who I am today and I am proud to embrace them.

My Recent Trip To The Motherland

In February 2026, my older sister and I flew to Puerto Rico to visit my uncle who had terminal cancer. The last time I had been to Puerto Rico was June 2015 with my dad. He unexpectedly passed away in 2018 from a heart attack, so this was my first trip back in 10 years, and first trip back without him. It was also my first time seeing Puerto Rico after Hurricane Maria, which changed so much of the island.

Reconnecting with all my family from my dad's side was the most meaningful part of the trip. I did not get a chance to grieve my dad with them, so a lot of emotions resurfaced as we visited all of his favorite spots. Spots that felt so familiar now felt different without him there. I found myself seeing Puerto Rico through my dad's eyes. I learned to appreciate the food, the music, the culture, and most importantly the people. The more I talked to people there, the more connected to Puerto Rico I felt. I finally felt like I belonged.

That trip truly changed my life. Puerto Rico isn't just the place where my dad was born, where my family is from, because now it feels like home to me too. Puerto Rico inspired me to make this website. My hope is that after you visit my site, you will want to visit Puerto Rico to learn why everyone has nicknamed is La Isla Del Encanto ("The Island of Enchantment"), and hopefully be inspired to experience it yourself.
